Michael Kiwanuka's stroke-delayed *Fudge* lands, a Belgian church organ gets treated as a synthesiser, Issy Wood paints herself at the NPG, and Susanna Clarke returns to Strange and Norrell's world.

## 📚 Books & Writing
- **Susanna Clarke — *The Bishop of Durham Attempts to Surrender the City*** — A short illustrated tale set 700 years before *Jonathan Strange & Mr Norrell*, in which a shrewd bishop tries to unite humans and fairies; Publishers Weekly compares the art to an illuminated manuscript.
  Read/Watch: [BookBrowse roundup of PW and Kirkus reviews](https://www.bookbrowse.com/bb_briefs/detail/index.cfm/ezine_preview_number/25985/the-bishop-of-durham-attempts-to-surrender-the-city%20jacket)

- **Kim Hye-jin — *Employee No. 9* (trans. Jamie Chang)** — A man is demoted to pressure him into early retirement and slowly absorbed by pointless work; a quiet Camus-ish fable of office life, strongly recommended by its reviewer.
