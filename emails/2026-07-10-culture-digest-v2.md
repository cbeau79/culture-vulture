# Culture Digest — 10 July 2026

A strong week for the long game: a double LP forty years in the making, a Guston show built out of poems, and Kelela turning R&B inside out on Warp.

## 🎵 Music

- **Kelela — *new avatar*** — Out today on Warp. Alt-R&B run through shoegaze distortion and industrial pulse, and somehow the softest thing she's made. Stereogum's Album of the Week.
  Read/Watch: [Album of the Week: Kelela's *new avatar*](https://stereogum.com/2504380/album-of-the-week-kelela-new-avatar/reviews/album-of-the-week)

- **Nirosta Steel — *My Skyscraper*** — Steven Hall spent forty years assembling this double LP of queer love songs and post-rave comedowns. He wrote with Arthur Russell until Russell's death in 1992; one of those songs is here.
  Read/Watch: [Nirosta Steel :: My Skyscraper](https://aquariumdrunkard.com/2026/07/06/nirosta-steel-my-skyscraper/)

- **Play Time — *Magic Object*** — Vida, Stardrum and Epstein on Moog, drums and sax, recorded over two days in a barn. Terry Riley's "In C" with a rhythm section, on the reliably esoteric Balmat.
  Read/Watch: [Play Time Play Hard](https://www.spinmagazine.com/2026/07/play-time-play-hard/)

- **mary in the junkyard — *Role Model Hermit*** — London trio's debut: shamanistic drums, orchestral strings, Clari Freeman-Taylor's ghostly soprano. Rock music done slightly wrong, to thrilling ends.
  Read/Watch: [NME review: a filmic debut](https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/mary-in-the-junkyard-role-model-hermit-review-3954289)

## 🎨 Art

- **Philip Guston's lines of poetry** — John Yau on the drawings Guston made in Woodstock as he abandoned abstraction, pared everything down to a line or two, and started again among poets.
  Read/Watch: [Philip Guston's Lines of Poetry](https://hyperallergic.com/philip-gustons-lines-of-poetry/)

- **Raven Chacon's resistance choirs** — Nate Wooley on the Diné composer's *Aviary*: field recordings of birds, and Chacon mimicking extinct species on instruments built from salvage.
  Read/Watch: [Resistance Choirs](https://www.nybooks.com/online/2026/06/26/resistance-choirs-raven-chacon/)

- **What the Statue of Liberty stands for** — Aruna D'Souza on artists who've subverted the monument, including Abigail DeVille's "Light of Freedom," which reimagines the torch as a symbol of protest.
  Read/Watch: [What Does the Statue of Liberty Stand For?](https://hyperallergic.com/p/f4e445e6-6611-4fb2-9090-c2825507c722/?member_status=free)

- **The artists countermapping the world** — Claudio Perna, Sandy Rodriguez and Firelei Báez reclaiming cartography as a medium for memory and migration.
  Read/Watch: [The Artists Countermapping the World](https://hyperallergic.com/the-artists-countermapping-the-world/)
