1. **Modern classical / ambient / minimalism** — Nils Frahm, Jon Hopkins, Helios, Chilly Gonzales. (The largest genre cluster: neoclassical, contemporary classical, minimalism, ambient. NB: this is *composed* ambient/instrumental music — distinct from sleep-noise, which is excluded.)

## Discovery preference

**Lean adventurous.** For the digest's music picks:
- Prioritise new releases and reissues from artists in or adjacent to the four strands above. No gigs or tours — this digest is for listening, not attending.
- Favour critically-acclaimed but lower-profile artists; a big mainstream release only earns a slot if genuinely significant.
- Light UK/Irish lean as a tie-breaker only (per the geographic focus), not a filter.
- At least half of each day's music picks should reflect these strands rather than generic new-release notability.
