# Reading Taste Profile

Derived from a full export of Chris's Goodreads library. The daily digest's **Books & Writing** section should weight toward this profile rather than picking purely by broad notability.

## The headline

A **substance-seeking reader** across fiction and big-idea non-fiction. Generous but discerning rater (mean 3.84★; favours depth). Strong pull toward literary classics, philosophy/meaning, and speculative fiction, with an emerging appetite for crime/noir.

## Four core strands

1. **Literary fiction & classics** — Fitzgerald (*The Great Gatsby*, 5★), Golding (*The Inheritors*, 5★), Camus (*The Stranger*), Salinger, Hemingway, Raymond Carver (short stories), Zadie Smith, Patrick deWitt, Dumas. Includes literary short fiction.
2. **Speculative fiction** — literary sci-fi (Ted Chiang *Stories of Your Life*, Orson Scott Card *Ender's Game*, both 5★) and epic fantasy (Joe Abercrombie's *First Law* — his top author, both loved; George R.R. Martin).
3. **Crime & noir** (emerging — from to-read shelf) — Michael Connelly (Bosch), James Ellroy, Ray Celestin. Historical and hard-boiled crime.
4. **Big-idea non-fiction**, four sub-veins:
   - *Philosophy, stoicism & meaning* — Marcus Aurelius *Meditations* (5★), Seneca, Viktor Frankl *Man's Search for Meaning* (5★), Camus.
   - *History & society* — Harari *Sapiens* (5★), Rosling *Factfulness*, Timothy Snyder *On Tyranny*.
   - *Systems thinking & economics* — Donella Meadows *Thinking in Systems*, Fritjof Capra, Alvin Roth *Who Gets What*.
   - *Creativity, focus & productivity* — Austin Kleon (top author; *Steal Like an Artist*, *Show Your Work*), Cal Newport *Digital Minimalism*, Sönke Ahrens *How to Take Smart Notes*, Covey. Plus personal-finance/FIRE (*The Simple Path to Wealth*, *Your Money or Your Life*).

## Top / repeat authors

Joe Abercrombie (fantasy), Austin Kleon (creativity). Currently reading: Brianna Wiest, Stephen Covey, George R.R. Martin (*Fire & Blood*).

## Era & format

Modern-dominant (2010s biggest by far) but with deep classic roots (Fitzgerald, Golding, Camus, Hemingway, Dumas, Shakespeare). Comfortable across novels, short-story collections, essays and long-form non-fiction.
